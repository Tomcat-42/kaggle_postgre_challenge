================================================================
KEY OBSERVATIONS
================================================================

1. FOREIGN KEY TRIGGERS ARE THE DOMINANT COST
   FK constraint validation accounts for 5,962 ms out of 12,599 ms total (47.3%).
   Every INSERT fires per-row FK triggers that look up the referenced table.
   At 100K rows, table1 alone spends 2,060 ms on FK checks (54.1% of its time).
   table6 is worst: 77.5% of its execution is FK triggers.

2. JSONB FIELD EXTRACTION IS THE SECOND LARGEST COST
   The CROSS JOIN LATERAL in recipe 06 (table2) expands 100K rows into 1.4M
   candidates by unpivoting 14 positions, then filters down to 232K. This
   JSONB extraction + Values Scan + filter costs 3,268 ms (57.4% of table2).
   Recipe 03 (table1) extracts ~15 JSONB fields per row at a cost of ~1,455 ms.

3. SEQUENTIAL SCANS ON raw_data ARE REPEATED 6 TIMES
   Every recipe does a full Seq Scan on raw_data (25,032 pages).
   With buffer caching, the I/O cost is only ~521 ms total across all 6 scans,
   but the JSONB parsing cost is paid fresh each time.

4. NO INDEX USAGE ON raw_data
   The index on (raw_schema, raw_id) is never used. The planner correctly
   chooses Seq Scan since raw_schema='group001' matches all 100K rows.
   At larger scales this remains optimal for full-table expansion.

5. BUFFER ACTIVITY
   Total buffers across all recipes:
     shared hit:  3,061,727
     shared read:    89,897
     dirtied:         8,495
     written:         8,620
   The large hit count (esp. recipes 04-06) comes from FK trigger lookups
   repeatedly probing the same index pages on table1.

6. PLANNER ESTIMATES ARE POOR
   PostgreSQL consistently underestimates row counts. For example, table1
   estimates 46,617 rows but gets 100,000. This is because the planner
   doesn't know the jsonb content distribution. This affects join strategy
   choices and hash table sizing (note "Buckets: 131072 originally 65536"
   resizing in several recipes).

7. RECIPE ORDERING IMPACT
   table3 and table4 (dimension tables) are cheap (~350 ms combined).
   table1 is the most expensive single recipe (3,811 ms) due to 15+ JSONB
   field extractions plus two FK triggers.
   table2 is the most expensive overall (5,689 ms) due to the 14-position
   CROSS JOIN LATERAL unpivot generating 232K rows with FK validation.
